The official website for the television anime adaptation of Nanai Umihoshi's Kizuguchi to Houtai (Wound and Bandage) manga revealed the main cast, a theme visual (pictured), and teaser promotional video on Friday. The anime series will premiere on January 9, 2027 at 11:00 p.m. on AT-X, followed by ABC TV and TV Asahi on the ANiMAZiNG programming block.
