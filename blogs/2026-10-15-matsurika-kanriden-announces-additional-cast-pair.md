The official website for the television anime adaptation of Rinne Ishida's Matsurika Kanriden (The Legend of Bureaucrat Matsurika) light novel announced an additional pair of cast on Thursday. The anime series is scheduled to premiere on Tokyo MX, MBS, TV Aichi, and BS11 in January 2027. Voice actors Hiyori Kouno (Kabushikigaisha Magi-Lumière) and Takuya Satou (Mao) are joining the cast as Ro Lian and Akatsuki, respectively.
