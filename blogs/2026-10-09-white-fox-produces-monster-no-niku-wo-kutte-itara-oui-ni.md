Production company King Records opened an official website for a television anime adaptation of Daken's Monster no Niku wo Kutte Itara Oui ni Tsuita Ken (How I Became King by Eating Monsters) light novel on Friday, revealing a teaser visual (pictured). The anime series is being produced by White Fox for a 2027 broadcast. Daken originally penned the adventure fantasy series on the Shoutsetsuka ni Narou website from January 2022 to January 2023.
