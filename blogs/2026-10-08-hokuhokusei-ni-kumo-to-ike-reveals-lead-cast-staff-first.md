The official website for the television anime adaptation of Aki Irie's Hokuhokusei ni Kumo to Ike (Go with the Clouds, North-by-Northwest) manga revealed the lead cast, main staff, a key visual (pictured), and the first promotional video on Thursday. The anime series will premiere on TBS in 2027 and will stream worldwide exclusively on Netflix. Voice actors Tomohiro Ono (Tomodachi Game) is starring as the lead character Kei Miyama.
