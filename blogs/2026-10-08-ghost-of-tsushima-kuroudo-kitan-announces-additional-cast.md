The Crunchyroll showcase at New York Comic Con 2026 revealed additional cast and staff for the anime adaptation of Sucker Punch Productions' Ghost of Tsushima video game on Thursday. The anime has been delayed to 2028 from its originally planned 2027 premiere. Mayumi Saco (Vinland Saga Season 2) and Shinya Fukumatsu (Tondemo Skill de Isekai Hourou Meshi) are joining the cast.
