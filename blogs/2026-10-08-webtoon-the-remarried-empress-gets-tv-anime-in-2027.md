The Crunchyroll Showcase at New York Comic Con 2026 announced a television anime adaptation of Alphatart and Sumpul's The Remarried Princess webtoon on Thursday. An official website was also launched, revealing the main cast, staff, a fourth character visual (pictured), and special promotional video. The anime series will premiere in 2027.
