Television broadcast company Fuji TV announced a live-action adaptation of Oreco Tachibana's Hotaru no Yomeiri (Firefly Wedding) web manga on Saturday, revealing an announcement promotional video. The live-action series is scheduled to premiere on Fuji TV in 2027. Tachibana began drawing the historical romance manga on the MangaONE platform in January 2023.
