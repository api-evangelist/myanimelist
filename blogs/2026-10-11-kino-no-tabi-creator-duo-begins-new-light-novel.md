Publishing company Kadokawa announced on Friday that author Keiichi Sigsawa and illustrator Kouhaku Kuroboshi, the duo behind the Kino no Tabi: The Beautiful World (Kino's Journey: The Beautiful World) light novel, will launch a new light novel titled Drifted: John Smith no Edo Hyouryuu Kitan. Mobile online game developer coly is credited for the original concept. The first volume will be published under the Dengeki Bunko imprint on November 10.
