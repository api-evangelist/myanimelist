Voice actors Kouji Yusa and Miyari Nemoto revealed their marriage through personal statements posted on social media on Thursday. Nemoto shared the news on her X account, writing, "It is with great humility that I announce my marriage registration with Kouji Yusa. Although I am still inexperienced, the two of us will support each other and continue to devote ourselves even more diligently going forward.
