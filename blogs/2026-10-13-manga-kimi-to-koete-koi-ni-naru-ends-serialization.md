Chihiro Yuzuki's Kimi to Koete Koi ni Naru (With You, Our Love Will Make It Through) manga ended its serialization on Wednesday. The 133th and final chapter marked the end of its five-year run. Yuzuki began serializing the supernatural romance manga, also abbreviated as Kimikoe, on the Manga Mee service in May 2021.
