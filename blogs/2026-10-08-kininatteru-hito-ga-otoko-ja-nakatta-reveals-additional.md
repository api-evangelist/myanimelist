The Crunchyroll Showcase at New York Comic Con 2026 revealed additional cast, staff, and a promotional video for the television anime adaptation of Sumiko Arai's Kininatteru Hito ga Otoko ja Nakatta (The Guy She Was Interested in Wasn't a Guy at All) manga on Thursday. The anime is scheduled to premiere on January 8 at 11:00 p.m. on NTV's Friday Anime Night timeslot.
