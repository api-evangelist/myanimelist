The official website for the television anime adaptation of Nao Iwamoto's Marronnier Oukoku no Shichinin no Kishi (The Seven Knights of the Marronnier Kingdom) manga announced the additional cast on Thursday. The anime premiered on October 3 at 6.25 p.m. on NHK E-Tele.
