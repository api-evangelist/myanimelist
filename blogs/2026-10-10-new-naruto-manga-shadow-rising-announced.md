The Naruto: What's Next? panel at New York Comic Con 2026 announced on Saturday that Masashi Kishimoto will launch a new Naruto manga titled Naruto: Shadow Rising, revealing a super teaser announcement video. The new series is part of Project Rasengan, a three-year timeline of upcoming releases within the Naruto franchise.
