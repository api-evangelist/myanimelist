The official website for the television anime adaptation of Makoto Morishita's Sudachi no Maoujou (The Fledgling Demon Lord's Starter Shop) manga unveiled an additional main cast member, key visual (pictured), and second teaser promotional video on Tuesday. The anime is scheduled to premiere in January 2027 on TOKYO MX, MBS, BS11 and other stations. Youhei Azakami (Kamonohashi Ron no Kindan Suiri 2nd Season) is joining the cast as Ash.
