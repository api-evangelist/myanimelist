The official website for the television anime adaptation of Ichi Yukishiro's Hone Dragon no Mana Musume (The Skull Dragon's Precious Daughter) web manga revealed the main cast, a key visual (pictured), and the first promotional video on Thursday. The anime series will premiere in 2027. Voice actors Kana Hanazawa (Angel Beats!) and Kazuhiko Inoue (Natsume Yuujinchou) are starring as Eve and Nemu, respectively.
