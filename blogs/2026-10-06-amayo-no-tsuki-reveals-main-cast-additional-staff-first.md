The official website for the television anime adaptation of Kuzushiro's Amayo no Tsuki (The Moon on a Rainy Night) manga revealed the main cast, additional staff, and the first promotional video on Wednesday. The anime is scheduled to premiere in 2027. Voice actresses Sora Amamiya (Nijusseiki Denki Mokuroku) and Iori Saeki (Pon no Michi) are starring in the anime as Kanon Oikawa and Saki Kindaichi, respectively.
